Privacy policy — T Level Engineering Core

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

The T Level Engineering Core app does not collect, store on any server or share any personal data.

What the app keeps on your phone

When you first open the app you type your name and, if you want to, your group. As you use the app it records which activities you have completed and your scores in tests and mock exams. This information is kept only in the app's storage on your own device so that you can see it under My results. It is never sent to the developer, your tutor or anyone else.

If your phone's own Android backup is switched on, Android may include this app's data in your personal device backup so that your results move with you to a new phone. That backup is managed by your phone and your Google account, not by this app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No adverts
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No internet access
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files

The app works fully offline and has no permission to use the internet.

Deleting your data

You can change your name in My results → Edit my details. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's Settings → Apps → T Level Engineering Core → Storage.
