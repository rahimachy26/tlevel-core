T Level Engineering Core helps learners on the T Level in Engineering and Manufacturing revise the core content, unit by unit.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• All 17 core units — from engineering representations, maths and science, materials and mechanical principles to electrical principles, mechatronics, control systems, quality, health and safety, business, stock control, continuous improvement and project management
• Key learning points and flip cards for every key term
• Interactive activities: matching, sorting, putting steps in order, calculations with worked answers, and extended-response questions with model answers
• 340 multiple-choice practice questions with explanations — take a quick 10-question test or the full 20 for each unit
• Full mock exams mixing questions from every unit, with an optional timer and a score breakdown by unit
• A searchable glossary of key terms
• My results: your best score for each unit and a history of every test you've taken

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-in and no adverts. You just enter your name once. Your results are saved only on your own phone and are never sent anywhere.

Made by a T Level tutor for T Level learners. The content is original revision material written to follow the T Level Engineering and Manufacturing Core specification.
